Find the Frog

One frog is hiding in every colored pond. Find them all.

1 frog per color1 frog per row and columnFrogs cannot touch each other (all 8 cells around a frog)

Tap a cell once to pencil a light X (your note: no frog here), tap again to place the frog, tap again to clear. A placed frog automatically crosses out its row, column, the 8 cells around it and its pond with dark X marks. A frog placed on a wrong cell costs a heart. Three hearts per game.
